# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Three kinds of visitor, each evaluating Saksham for a different reason, and the site
must serve all three rather than over-index on one:

- Recruiters and hiring managers screening for software engineering internships and roles.
  They usually skim first, so the home page has to hand over the facts (who he is, headline
  numbers, flagship projects, stack, how to reach him) in a fast top-to-bottom scan.
- Fellow engineers and the developer community, who judge the craft itself and the technical
  depth behind the projects.
- Startup founders and small eng teams hiring for range and ownership, who care that he can
  ship end-to-end across machine learning, embedded systems, and full-stack.

Their shared job to be done: quickly understand who Saksham is and what he can build, then
decide to reach out. Success is a visitor leaving with a clear, credible impression and
starting a conversation about working together.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Saksham Srivastava (Computer Engineering B.S. '27, University of
South Florida). It frames him as a one-person design-and-engineering bureau and makes his
creative-technical range legible and believable, turning interest into contact. It exists
because his range is unusual and easy to flatten into a generic resume; the site's job is to
show it as one coherent identity and prove it with real shipped work.

## Positioning

A rare creative-technical hybrid: an engineer who lives where art and engineering overlap,
across on-device machine learning, music, generative math, and high-availability systems,
with real shipped work behind every claim. That is the single idea every screen reinforces.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: the work is the hook and contact is the conversion, wired the
  industry-standard way. The hero leads with "View Projects" and keeps Contact one click
  away everywhere; the projects then funnel to the contact page as the conversion endpoint.
  Secondary paths for visitors not ready to reach out: the live GitHub feed and the deeper
  project pages.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a creative-technical hybrid who ships real
  things, from on-device ML to a platform that served 40,000+ people.
- Belief ladder: (1) this person has genuine craft and taste (the site itself is the first
  proof); (2) the technical depth and range are real (ML, embedded, systems, math); (3) he
  ships things that matter under real pressure, not just demos; (4) he would be a strong hire
  or collaborator, so it is worth reaching out.
- Proof on hand: 40,000+ people routed to care at ~99.9% uptime (COVID-HELP); ~30 ms/frame
  on-device inference on a Raspberry Pi (MoodScope); an AI DJ across 17 blend strategies and
  100+ audio features (AutoMix); 21 merged open-source PRs; a live GitHub feed; Green & Gold
  Presidential Scholar; undergraduate researcher at USF's Bellini College of AI.

## Brand Personality

Inventive, precise, and unmistakably his own. The voice is a confident builder with taste and
a dry sense of humor, comfortable being technical without being dry. It should feel like a
person, not a company. Emotionally the site aims for intrigue and "who made this?" respect
backed by immediate credibility, and never at the expense of clarity.

## Anti-references

Never hard to read or overdesigned. Cleverness that costs legibility is the one failure the
owner named explicitly; AA contrast and a large body size are floors, not trade-offs. It also
must not fall back into the muted, interchangeable SaaS-template look the site was
deliberately rebuilt away from; the current КБ-14 Soviet-constructivist / brutalist system is
the committed identity, and future work should deepen it rather than sand it back toward safe.

## Design Principles

Readability is the floor. No amount of style justifies text that is hard to read; every
decision is checked against legibility first.

Craft as evidence. The site itself is the strongest proof of ability, so every detail is
hand-built and nothing is templated.

Show the range as one identity. The ML, music, generative-art, and systems breadth is
presented as a single coherent thesis, not a scattered list of unrelated skills.

Proof over adjectives. Real numbers and shipped work carry the claims; buzzwords do not.

One site, three readers. Fast facts for recruiters, depth for engineers, and evidence of
range for founders, delivered through standard portfolio patterns so no audience is left out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA: body text meets >=4.5:1 contrast and large text >=3:1, body copy is
>=18px, and all generative art is kept off text so nothing competes with reading. Motion
honors prefers-reduced-motion with static or crossfade fallbacks throughout, and interactive
controls are keyboard-operable with visible focus states.
